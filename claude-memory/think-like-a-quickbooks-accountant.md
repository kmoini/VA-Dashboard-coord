Amin, 2026-11-23, after I asked him to ask the accounting team which account "due from a related company" is and whether a mirror case exists: "these questions are a waste of time". The team only describes how THEY work for THIS company; the product serves many clients with their own accountants and their own company structures.

**Why:** a rule built from one team's answer is one client's convention hard-coded into a general product.

**How to apply:** decide the accounting treatment myself from standard QuickBooks forms and practice (if unsure, use another agent or look it up, or give Amin a prompt for a Claude coworker to check QuickBooks). Whatever differs between clients (account names, one account per company or not) is read from the client's own chart or registry and confirmed once by the accountant in the product, never asked of the team. My interpretation: factual questions about a specific document or bank line are still fine to ask Amin.
